CORE-BRIEFS / Publication 7 / 2026-11-09 / approved 2026-11-11

One combined snapshot, one meeting brief, one routine refresh

The finalized Budget Snapshot draft and the Treasurer Notes become ordinary outputs of uv run np-finance end-to-end-run, next to Classroom Budgets. Fundraising vs. Obligations stops being a separate document. Every financial figure uses one as-of date.

What I heard

  • Combine the two financial briefs: start from the Budget Snapshot and move in the obligations, scenarios and assumptions from Fundraising vs. Obligations.
  • The headline boxes are bank accounts (checking, time account and the credit card), raised, spent, spending still ahead and a projected year-end balance.
  • Remove the unknown-actuals box, everything from Largest budget lines down, the page-1 obligations tables, Fundraising versus the plan, the reserves strip and the fine print.
  • Put progress first, with no time-of-year markers and a projected segment for upcoming events.
  • Rename the old central case to Minimum self-funding and make last year's pace the Central Case.
  • Use October 31 for everything. Specialist contracts paid at the start of the year are this year's expense and already paid; next year's contracts fall after June 30.
  • Warn in red when projected checking falls below the floor needed for next year's contracts.
  • Add the Treasurer Notes to the standard documents.

Why this shape

Step 65 already captures the roster, the school-year ledger, the prior-year budget and the dated balances through the normal command, so the redesigned snapshot only needs new facts and a new template on the same seam. The Treasurer Notes reads the same facts as the snapshot, so the two cannot disagree. The board questions stay yours to write, but any number inside them is filled from the run instead of typed by hand. The set stays at seven reports; only its membership changes.
